Why OmniLoop

Divi can list posts. This turns the list into a shop.

The free Post Filter is a list with checkboxes. OmniLoop is the catalog on top: fast filters, cards you design, merchandising, and URLs Google can read — still one Visual Builder module.

01

One module does the whole page

Filters, grid, sort, and page numbers live together. Nested Card Items let you reorder image, title, price, and buttons in the layers panel.

02

Stays fast as the catalog grows

Queries hit OmniLoop’s own index, not a stack of WordPress meta joins. Save a product — that row updates. Rebuild once when you change what you index.

03

WooCommerce is optional

Loop posts or any public type on Divi + WordPress alone. Add Woo later for swatches, price slider, stock, sale, rating, cart, and quick view.

04

Filter pages Google can read

Navy + bags can look like /ol/color/navy/category/bags/. You control title, heading, and whether page 2 is hidden from search.

05

Pin, promote, hide empty shelves

Search to pin a hero product. Push sold-out items last. Promo badges and “Only 2 left” come from Settings — no extra plugin.

06

Keep Divi’s Loop if you already designed it

Native Loop attach hands matching products to Divi’s own loop. Other loops on the page stay untouched unless you opt them in.

Divi Post Filter A free list that filters. No custom index, merchandising, card builder, or pretty SEO URLs.
Woo-only filter plugins Strong on products. Awkward if you also loop posts, or want a native Divi 5 module.
OmniLoop for Divi One Divi 5 module. Own index. Cards you design. Merch and SEO. Woo when you want it.
